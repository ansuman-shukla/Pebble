# Plan: the globe (M3 world generation + M4 planet viewer)

Shared contract for everyone building the round world. Read with `docs/design/01-world.md`, `07-spectator-client.md`,
`09-roadmap.md` (M3, M4 rows) and `docs/decisions.md`. Goal: open http://localhost:5180/ and see the whole planet
(oceans, continents, biomes, rivers, poles, the volcano…), zoom down onto the castle town, and walk in third person
from the town out into the wild, around the planet, past camps and landmarks.

## Frame and units
- Metres. Planet centre at the origin. **+Y = north pole.** three.js right-handed.
- Unit direction from latitude/longitude: `u = (cos(lat)·sin(lon), sin(lat), cos(lat)·cos(lon))` (lon 0 at +Z, lon +90° at +X).
- Radius `R = 2000` (world spec). A surface point is `u · (R + h)`, `h` = height above sea level (negative = sea floor).
  The sea surface is the sphere of radius R.
- Tangent frame at `u`: `up = u`, `east = normalize(cross(Y, u))` (near a pole, |cross| < 1e-6, use `normalize(cross(Z, u))`),
  `north = cross(up, east)`, `south = -north`. Local three.js axes: **X = east, Y = up, Z = south** (east × up = south ✓),
  matching the town's local metres (x east, z south).

## Grid
- Subdivided icosahedron, frequency 60: **36,002 cells** (vertices; Goldberg dual: hexagons + 12 pentagons, ~40 m apart)
  and **72,000 triangles** whose corners are cells. The backend generates it once; the client never regenerates it,
  it uses the arrays it receives (so cell order is defined by the backend).

## Terrain and biomes
- Heights: land up to ~+250 m, the volcano peak ~+320 m, sea floor down to ~−120 m. Ocean ≈ 40 % of the surface.
- Temperature from latitude and height; moisture from noise + distance to sea; biome from both.
- **Biome ids (fixed order):** 0 `deep_ocean`, 1 `shallow_ocean`, 2 `beach`, 3 `grassland`, 4 `broadleaf_forest`,
  5 `taiga`, 6 `wetland`, 7 `hills`, 8 `mountain`, 9 `savanna`, 10 `tundra`, 11 `snow`, 12 `ice`, 13 `lake`, 14 `volcanic`.
  Water (not walkable) = 0, 1, 13. Everything else is walkable land (rivers are fordable in v0).
- **Land loop (required):** a closed ring of land around the planet (continents joined by land bridges), closed by the
  **ice gate**, a walkable ice bridge at high latitude. Authored as spine waypoints in the world spec; noise adds shape.
- Rivers: paths of cells flowing downhill from springs to the sea or a lake; the great falls is a river dropping off a
  cliff into a lake.
- Sites are flattened: the town to a plateau at `h0` (≈ +8 m) within 230 m of its anchor, blended out to 300 m
  (the town spec covers ±160 m); camps ~40 m; landmarks as their shape needs.

## Sites
Authored in `backend/data/world/world.yaml` by lat/lon (temperate river valley near the coast for the town; the two
camps near water, far from the town and from each other; landmarks per `01-world.md`):
`town` (the castle = landmark 1), `band_a_camp`, `band_b_camp`, `smoking_mountain` (volcano), `great_falls`,
`elder_tree`, `standing_stones`, `painted_cave` (near band A), `ice_gate` (on the land loop near a pole), `hot_springs`
(in the mountains). Each site has an anchor (cell, unit position, east, north, `height_m` = h0) and optional
**placements in local metres** in exactly the town placement format (`kind`, `x`, `z`, `rotation_deg` ∈ {0,90,180,270},
`footprint`, `walk`), using frontend registry kinds. The town's placements come from `/api/v1/town`.

## Local metres → globe (one mapping for the town and every site; it is gnomonic, so it inverts exactly)
- Forward: `v = up·(R+h0) + east·x + south·z`, `u' = normalize(v)`, position = `u'·(R + h0)` (+ the model's own base y
  along `u'`). Orientation: `up' = u'`, `east' = normalize(east − (east·u')·u')`, `south' = cross(east', up')`; basis
  columns (east', up', south'); then the placement's yaw `rotation_deg` about `up'` (three.js `rotation.y` convention).
- Inverse (world point p → local): `v = normalize(p)`, `t = (R+h0) / dot(v, up)`, `x = t·dot(v, east)`, `z = t·dot(v, south)`.
- Inside a site's plateau the ground is at `h0` (plus walkable surfaces such as the bridge); elsewhere it is the terrain.

## API: `GET /api/v1/world` → `WorldView` (gzip; exact names, the frontend client is generated from them)
```
class Anchor(BaseModel): position: tuple[float, float, float]; east: tuple[float, float, float]; north: tuple[float, float, float]; height_m: float
class Site(BaseModel): id: str; kind: Literal["town", "camp", "landmark"]; name: str; cell: int; anchor: Anchor; placements: list[TownPlacement] = []
class River(BaseModel): id: str; name: str | None = None; cells: list[int]
class WorldView(BaseModel):
    seed: int; generator_version: str; radius_m: float; frequency: int; cell_count: int
    biome_names: list[str]            # index = biome id
    positions: str                    # base64, little-endian float32, cell_count*3 unit vectors (x, y, z)
    heights: str                      # base64 float32, cell_count, metres above sea level
    biomes: str                       # base64 uint8, cell_count
    triangles: str                    # base64 uint32, triangle_count*3 cell indices (counter-clockwise seen from outside)
    rivers: list[River]
    land_loop: list[int]              # closed cycle of neighbouring walkable cells
    sites: list[Site]
```
Generated deterministically from (seed, spec); same seed + spec = byte-identical arrays. Persisted in Postgres (table
`worlds`: seed, generator_version, spec_hash, payload, created_at; first Alembic migration); generated on first request
if missing (and by `python -m app.world.generate`). Generation target < 30 s.

## Client rules (M4)
- Terrain: one mesh from the arrays (vertex colours by biome, light height shading, low-poly flat look), ocean sphere at
  R (+ shallow tint), rivers as strips, poles snow/ice, the volcano visible from far with a smoke plume.
- Sun: directional light circling the planet with a client game clock, 1 real s = 1 game minute (day = 24 real
  minutes), starting at 08:00 town time; real day and night sides; dusk colours; fire light at camps and lanterns in town
  at night (cheap point lights or emissive). v0 has no axial tilt.
- Cameras: `orbit` (whole planet, zoom smoothly from ~7000 m down to the surface), `follow` (third person, camera up =
  local normal), `fly`. `C` cycles; zooming the orbit camera down onto the player hands over to follow.
- Props: biome vegetation/rocks scattered deterministically per cell on the client (hash of seed + cell) from a
  biome → kinds/density table (`frontend/src/lib/world/biome-props.ts`), instanced, only within ~600 m of the camera
  target, rebuilt as the player moves; far away the terrain colours carry the look. Sites render their placements with
  the mapping above; the town renders its full placement set.
- Walking on the globe: move in the local tangent plane, re-project onto the sphere, ground height from the terrain mesh
  (barycentric lookup in the triangle under the player) or the site plateau; water biomes block; inside the town the
  existing town collisions apply in local metres via the inverse mapping.
- Budget: 60 fps on an M-series Mac in follow and orbit, < 300 draw calls, initial assets ≤ 30 MB.

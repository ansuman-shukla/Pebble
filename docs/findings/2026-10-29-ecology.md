# Findings: ecology (M6, 2026-10-29)

Animals, predators, fish and fire in the simulation, the actions that go with them (`hunt`, `fish`, `make_fire`,
`feed_fire`, `attack`, a real `flee`), the scripted brains that use them, and the animals and fires drawn live on the
planet. Numbers are from the stored world of generator `m3.2` (seed 20261028) on an M-series Mac.

- ~270 animals at the start (seed 20261028: 273). Kind names are the frontend registry's (`ANIMALS`).
- Groups settle near the town and both camps first (herbivores 200-700 m out, predators 600-1600 m), the rest
  anywhere in their habitat, 500 m apart. A group's size is its soft cap: a dead member's remains stay until its slot
  respawns (1-6 game days by kind) as one `animal_spawned` event with `replaces`.

```
 per tick: group goals ─▶ each living animal (by id)
   herbivore: person (not stalking its group) within flee range, or awake predator within 40 m ─▶ run 2 minutes away
              night ─▶ sleep │ far from the group's goal ─▶ walk back │ else graze 20-60 min
   predator:  quarry? ─▶ chase (10 min at most) ─▶ close: bite the person / 20-40 % to bring the animal down
              sun < +4° and hungry ─▶ exposed person within 40 m, else prey within 150 m │ day ─▶ sleep
 ─▶ straight steps towards the goal (blocked: step aside) ─▶ respawn the dead whose time has come
```

- People are safe within 15 m of a lit fire, with two others within 10 m, inside the town's walls, and by day.
  A bite injures by kind (wolf 12-25, bear 25-40, lion 20-35, white bear 30-45); each bite ends the attack with a
  50 % chance (at most three). A kill or a death feeds the predator.
- Animals never enter the town chart, never walk onto the wrong ground (swimmers stay in their water) and keep 25 m
  from a lit fire (a step out of a new fire's circle is always allowed).

## Fire (`backend/app/sim/fire.py`)

- Hearths: both camps' fire pits (burning, 6 h of fuel, at the start of a run, a woodpile of 12 beside each, 3.2 m
  out between the seats) and the town's four fire baskets (cold). Lanterns aren't fires.
- A piece of wood burns 60 minutes; a fire holds 12 hours. Levels: out, embers (≤ 30 min), low (≤ 2 h), burning.
  Lighting, feeding and every change of level is one `fire_changed`; campfires made anywhere get `fire:<n>` ids.
- A lit fire gives night sight 30 m within 12 m (forest still ×0.4), roasts raw meat and fish eaten within 4 m
  (×1.5 food; raw has a 30 % chance to make you sick: an `injured` with cause `bad food`) and keeps animals away.

## The actions

| Action | Rule |
|---|---|
| `hunt(animal)` | an animal in view (by kind or group words); creep up at 50 m a minute (its group doesn't notice), strike within 15 m: chance = kind × skill (×0.4 without "hunting") × spear ×1.4 × √strength × (1 + 0.25 per other hunter after the same group within 30 m, max 2), at most 0.9. A kill: meat and hide (`item_moved` from the animal); a miss scatters the group and boar/elk/predators may turn on you. An hour without closing in: "You lose the trail." |
| `fish()` | at a river or beside a lake or shallow sea (deep sea: "too deep to fish without a boat"); 30 min; 35 % by hand (spear ×1.4, hunting skill ×1.3) for 1-3 fish from the water's stock, which grows back in ~4 days |
| `make_fire()` | skill "fire" and wood carried or lying beside the fire; lights a hearth within 4 m (10 min, 2 pieces) or a campfire where you stand (never elsewhere in town) |
| `feed_fire()` | a lit fire within 4 m; wood carried first, then its woodpile (within 5 m of the fire), up to full |
| `attack(target)` | a person within reach (a blow, cause `violence`) or an animal in view (stalk to 3 m, a wound; a predator that survives is driven off and may bite back) |
| `flee()` | away from the nearest dangerous animal in view, 150 m at 1.6× pace (turning aside from water and walls) |
| `pick_up(item)` | town folk can take from a storehouse within reach (the guard's firewood) |

Perception lists animals by group ("a herd of deer some way off to the east (grazing)", "a wolf close by to the
west (watching you)"); interrupts: an animal group coming into view, a predator coming at you ("You stop gathering: a
wolf is coming at you from the trees, close by to the north."), and being hurt, which wakes sleepers ("You wake: a
wolf attacks you!").

## The brains

- Hunters (the hunting skill: both leaders and both hunters) hunt in the morning: game in view is stalked, else up to
  three searches around camp; meat goes home and is roasted at the fire at supper.
- Bands feed the camp fire once each at dusk (so it lasts the night), tend it if awake and it burns low, and those
  with the skill relight it; the gatherers' daily chore is now wood (the camp burns about a dozen pieces a night).
  Foragers at a river or lake fish now and then.
- Kenji (now with the fire skill) fetches wood from the storehouse at dusk (300 pieces in store) and fills both main
  gate baskets; they burn through the night.
- Anyone who sees a dangerous animal coming at them or watching them runs, unless a fire is beside them or they're
  inside the walls.

## Costs

- Simulation tick with ~270 animals and the 15 citizens: 1.3 ms mean without the database (0.3 ms before animals);
  the animal step is vectorised (numpy) for who sees whom and who runs from whom.
- Events: still ~6 000 a game day; animal poses ride in the tick's `tick_summary` (only animals that changed, ~40 a
  tick), which grows to ~2.4 kB stored (compressed). Snapshots ~106 kB stored. The engine writes the hourly snapshot
  tick in ~30 ms.
- The feed sends every animal and fire in each `tick` message (~270 animals, ~70 kB of JSON at ×1).

## Spectator view

- `GET /entities`, `/entities?at=` and the live `snapshot`/`tick` messages carry `animals` (id, kind, group,
  position, heading_deg, activity: idle, grazing, walking, running, hunting, sleeping, dead) and `fires` (id, kind
  pit/basket/campfire, position, lit, strength 0-1). Citizen activities gained running (flee), hunting, fishing and
  tending_fire.
- The globe draws animals within 400 m of the camera with their registry model (recoloured stand-ins), Idle / Walk /
  Run clips (fish and sharks: Idle / Swim / Swim fast), gliding a tick at the feed's speed; remains lie on their side.
  Lit fires flicker (flame cones + a glow sprite) within 600 m and the three nearest cast warm light.
- The mock feed (`?mockLive=1`) has a deer herd and a fox on the town's approach, deer and two wolves by band A's
  camp, both camp fires burning and the main gate's baskets lit from 18:30 to 06:00; `__pebbleMockLive.skip(n)` jumps
  ahead for tests.

## Known limits and open points

- Animals step in straight lines between goals; one whose way is blocked steps aside at random, so after a long flight
  across water a few strays (~3 % after three days) sit beyond their range for a while.
- No occlusion: town folk see (and are interrupted by) animals beyond the walls; the brains know the walls keep them
  out and don't run.
- Predators aren't hunted by anyone yet and never starve; sharks only patrol the deep sea.
- Food doesn't spoil and isn't shared; the hunters eat their own meat.
- Warmth from fire waits for seasons (v1); lanterns aren't fires.
- Hunting success is high when a strike happens (a spear and a helper give ~0.8 on deer) but many hunts end with the
  trail lost; worth tuning when agents hunt.

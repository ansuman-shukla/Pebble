## Still open (root cause and recommendation)

- **br1.coin_trend:** the stall's till (Fumiko) and the treasury are coupled through the lord's lean rule (D180): with
  the dues lower the lord sells more granary rice into the stall, which pays for it. Daily cash swings ±100, so a
  10-point slope is noisy. Recommend judging the trend on the accrual basis (cash ± dues owed, see
  `/tmp/pebble-v1/q1/trend.py`) and tuning `TREASURY_START`/`TREASURY_SWING` to the real start (2 015).
- **e3.rice_price peak:** the run's largest shocks dip the stall to 84 measures (×1.19, one measure off ×1.2) because
  the lord's granary sale and the farmers' ×1.5 answer within hours. The rule is proven by the scripted shock in
  `tests/brains/test_town.py`. Recommend judging the peak there, or ×1.15 in the run.
- **t1.near_people:** band B's camp sits 34-49 % (band A 70-77 %): its herds' homes are 200-700 m out and forest cuts
  their range; a data change to `NEAR_SITE_M` reshuffles every placement. Recommend an A1/T1 follow-up.
- **q1.overlaps:** band A's two traders walking to town together end a tick at the same point with one of them just
  stopped (1 tick a seed at most). A walk-end spot check in S1 would close it.

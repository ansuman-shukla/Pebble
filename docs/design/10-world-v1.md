# 10 · World v1 (the bible)

**World v1 makes the world real before any mind enters it:** every usable thing is drawn where it is and simulated
where it is drawn; places cluster into hotspots linked by roads; bodies move at real speeds, take real spots and
never merge; animals visibly graze, flee, stalk and kill; a copper-coin economy with minerals, a market, tax,
ownership and theft gives everyone something to get and something to lose; a small written law, ranks and standing
make the deeds matter. Phase B (`04-minds.md`) starts only after the World v1 critic checklist
(`plans/2026-10-29-world-v1.md`) passes.

This doc owns the rules of World v1. Where an older design doc says otherwise, this one wins (decisions D77-D96).
The auditor's fault catalogue of the M9 world (F1-F22, `.runtime/briefs/audit-1.md`) is answered here and mapped to
streams in the plan. Numbers are v1 defaults; they live in data files (`backend/data/*.yaml`) so the balance run can tune them within
±30 % without a design change (a larger change is a decision row).

Principles (unchanged from 00, sharpened): the world is the authority (everything enforced in sim code, never in
prompts); minds get exactly the actions and perceptions scripted brains use; every state change is one event;
**visible = simulated**; something must always be happening (needs, scarcity, ambition, threats).

## 1. Places and resources

### 1.1 Nodes: every usable wild thing is a thing with an id
A **node** = `id, kind, hotspot | None, position, cell, stock, cap, stage, regrow_at, spots[1-4]`. Per-cell resource
counters (D56, `sim/items.py RESOURCES`) are removed. Two sources, one table (`sim/nodes.py`):

- **Flora** (generated by the backend from the seed, `world/flora.py`): every tree, berry bush, nut tree, root patch,
  reed bed and deadwood pile (fallen logs, branches, driftwood, **dead trees**) on the planet by biome density
  (≈ 42 k trees, ≈ 6 k others). Served once as `GET /api/v1/world/flora` (packed binary: cell u32, offset 2×i8 dm,
  kind u8, scale u8, yaw u8; ≈ 400 kB, cached). The client draws every usable kind **only** from this table.
- **Fixed features** (authored per hotspot, `data/world/hotspots.yaml`): mineral seams, fishing spots, paddies, dens,
  waterholes, signposts, the town's reed clumps on its moat and stream.

- **Reach:** gathering, fishing, felling, mining and farming need the actor within **3 m of a free spot** of a node
  with stock. A spot is claimed while the action runs (one claim registry with bodies' slots, §4.3).
  `walk_to(thing)` resolves to the nearest free spot of the nearest matching node with stock and arrives facing it.
  Failures say why in-world: "The nearest berry bush is a few steps away; go to it first.", "Someone is already
  picking that bush.", "The water here is too fast and shallow for fish."
- **Felling:** `gather("wood")` takes deadwood in reach first; with an axe carried and no deadwood, it fells the
  standing tree whose spot you hold: 10 logs land as a ground stack at the trunk, `tree_felled {node, yaw}`.
- One stage table `data/world/node_stages.yaml` gives both the perception words ("a berry bush heavy with fruit",
  "a stripped berry bush", "a stump") and the renderer's stage, so what is said is what is drawn.
- Snapshots store only nodes that differ from full; the live feed carries the node stages that changed this tick.

### 1.3 Roads, paths and bridges (`world/roads.py`, part of the world view)
- Graph: town gates, camps, hotspot centres, landmarks; routed by Dijkstra on the cell graph over terrain cost, most
  important first; each routed cell is discounted to 0.35× for later routes so they merge into trunks.
- **Roads** (3 m, gravel ruts): main gate → North woods, Gulf shore, South marsh; west gate → Lord's woods → band B;
  west gate → Standing stones → Hot springs; main gate → Elder grove → Falls pool; spur to the obsidian field.
  **Paths** (1.5 m, trodden earth): the land loop ("the great trail"), each camp to its hotspots and to the loop.
- Sim cost: road cell `max(0.8, base × 0.5)`, path cell `max(0.9, base × 0.7)`; a road or path over a river is a
  **bridge** (cost 0.8, drawn log-plank bridge) instead of the 1.5 ford. A* then prefers roads with no new movement
  code. A **signpost** stands at every road junction (names the two places). Perception: "You are on the road from
  the castle town to the Elder grove."

### 1.4 Rice paddies (replace `FARM_GROWTH` and the virtual field store)
- `work("farm")` at a paddy spot = 60 min tending, **+34 growth** (elders ×0.6); one counted hour per paddy per day.
  100 growth → ripe (stock 60). `gather("rice")` at a ripe paddy harvests ≤ 10 per 20 min (15 with a sickle). Empty →
  stubble; `work("farm")` on stubble replants (seedlings, growth 0). An untended ripe paddy loses 2 a day.
- Start growth staggered 0/20/40/60/80/100 → up to 120 measures a day (1 200 food) against the town's 11 people ×
  100 = 1 100 food a day. Farmers only ever stand inside the paddies.

### 1.5 Territory (`sim/territory.py`)
- **Home ground:** town chart + 400 m; each camp 400 m. Owned by that group; one constant `HOME_GROUND_M = 400`.
- **Claimed hotspots** are static in v1 (table 1.2): Lord's woods (town), Eastern wall scar (band A). A claimed
  hotspot shows its marker: town boundary stone with a banner, band cairn with a hide streamer.
- Taking from a node, hunting or fishing on another group's ground or claimed hotspot **succeeds** and is a
  `trespassed` deed (§3.3). In the Lord's woods, town folk may gather but only the lord and samurai may hunt.
- Items lying on the ground are finders-keepers (no ground-stack owners in v1).

## 2. Economy

### 2.1 Money: the mon
- One coin, the **mon** (copper, holed). Item `coins`, counted, weight 0.004 kg. Said as "a coin", "seven coins"
  (2-12 in words), "a short string of coins" (13-99), "N strings of coins" (100s). Coin counts are in-world knowledge
  (D63 bans ids and ranges, not what a person can count).
- **Closed supply: 3 000 mon**, never minted or destroyed in v1. Start: lord's treasury (keep chest) 2 015, merchant
  500, every other adult town citizen 60 (8 × 60), the child 5, bands 0. Invariant tested every tick: coins in
  inventories + stores + ground = 3 000.
- Bands see and use coins like anyone (no money-concept learning in v1).

### 2.4 Wear and spoilage (`sim/wear.py`)
- **Spoilage at each holder's local dawn**: raw meat and fish −50 % (ceil), berries −30 %, roots −10 %; rice, nuts,
  dried meat, cloth, minerals, tools never. One `spoiled {holder, items}` event per holder per dawn.
- **Tools break** per use: spear 4 % per hunt strike, axe 2 % per felling, sickle 1.5 % per harvest session; stone ×2.
  `tool_broke`. No per-piece wear state.

### 2.5 The market stall (`sim/market.py`)
- The drawn market stall is a store owned by the merchant, **open while the merchant works `trade` within 6 m**;
  `buy`/`sell` fail otherwise ("The stall is shut.").
- **Price = base × clamp(√(target / (stock + 1)), 0.5, 2.0)**, rounded up to whole mon per unit (small goods trade in
  lots: berries/nuts 10, roots/reeds 2). The stall sells at the price and **buys at 75 %**, only what it can pay
  for and up to 2 × target. Targets: rice 120, wood 40, hide 6, cloth 6, dried meat 20, fish 10, meat 10, flint 10,
  obsidian 6, iron sand 20, gold 10, each tool 2. Seed stock: rice 60, cloth 2, dried meat 10, wood 20, one spear.
- At the stall a citizen perceives each item as "dear / fair / cheap" + the price in words. `prices_changed` when an
  item's word changes.
- No person-to-person barter action in v1: swaps are agreed in speech and done with two `give`s.

| Store | Owner | Reach | Notes |

**Theft:** `pick_up` from a store you don't own **succeeds** and emits `stole {thief, owner, store, item, count,
witnesses}` (witnesses = citizens who had the thief in view that tick). The owner notices at the store next time
("Some of your rice is gone.") without learning who. No pickpocketing.

### 2.7 Tax, wages and the money loop (`sim/tax.py`)
- **Rice tax 40 % of each paddy harvest** (nengu): `owed += ⌈0.4 × count⌉` on the harvester's household at each
  `gather("rice")`; `work("store food")` at the granary pays it down first (`tax_paid`). Unpaid at local midnight →
  `tax_overdue {household, owed}`; arrears carry over; 3 days overdue lets the lord judge `confiscate` (§3.4).
- **Wages** are not a rule; they are the lord giving coins (scripted: 15 mon at dusk to Kenji and Goro; 30 mon per
  tool ordered from the smith). The lord sells granary surplus (> 3 days of household food) at the stall every 2 days.
- Loop: treasury → wages/orders → stall → farmers, smith, digger, bands → stall → treasury.
- Daily `ledger` event per citizen at local midnight: coins, wealth, income/spend 24 h, tax owed (cheap series for
  the scoreboard).

### 3.2 Groups, ranks, titles
- `citizen.group` ∈ {town, band_a, band_b} (today's `region`). Titles are static in v1: lord, samurai, ashigaru,
  leader. **Rank**: lord 5; samurai, monk, band leader 4; ashigaru, merchant, smith, elders 3; adults and teens 2;
  children 1.
- Permissions the world checks: `judge` needs `lord` at the castle or `leader` within 15 m of the camp fire pit;
  hunting in the Lord's woods needs `lord` or `samurai`.
- **Succession:** the lord's death makes the highest-ranked samurai lord. **Band leadership by standing:** at each
  local dawn, if an adult's band standing ≥ leader's + 25 on 3 dawns running, or the leader died, the highest-standing
  adult becomes leader (`leader_changed`).

### 3.3 Law: deeds the world detects (`data/society/laws.yaml`)
| Deed | World rule | Event |
|---|---|---|
| theft | `pick_up` from a store you don't own | `stole` |
| trespass | outsider (group ≠ town) inside the walls between the dusk and dawn bells (sunset/sunrise if nobody rings); anyone but lord/samurai/ashigaru inside the castle walls at night | `trespassed {kind: walls \| castle}` (once per entry) |
| poaching | taking from a node, hunting or fishing on another group's home ground or claimed hotspot, or hunting in the Lord's woods without the title | `trespassed {kind: ground, took: {item, count}}` |
| assault / killing | `attack` on a person | existing `injured` / `died` with cause `violence` and the attacker |

- **One emitter per deed:** `stole` from `sim/ownership.py`, every `trespassed` from `sim/territory.py`. Society
  keeps a deed log keyed by the event's seq.
- **Witnesses** = citizens whose visibility set that tick contains the doer (normal sight: day 150 m, night 20 m,
  forest ×0.4). Witnesses know the deed at once (`known_deeds`); others only through `accuse`. Unwitnessed deeds stay
  in the log for spectators ("got away with it").
- **Sanctuary:** nobody can be seized within 22 m of the temple. **Children** (< 13) can only be pardoned.

### 3.4 Enforcement (`sim/justice.py`)
| Action | Kind | Rule |
|---|---|---|
| `accuse(person, to)` | instant | passes every deed you know of `person` to `to` (within speech range, awake); `to` learns them as hearsay ("Goro says Kaz stole rice."). Lies stay free speech (`say`) |
| `seize(person)` | durative 1 min, reach 2 m | p = clamp(0.5 + 0.3·(armed_s − armed_t) + 0.3·(str_s − str_t) + 0.2 per armed helper within 3 m, 0.1, 0.95). Success → target `held`: only look/check/say/wait/flee; walks led 1 m behind the seizer at 0.8× speed. Held `flee` succeeds 15 % per try (+25 % if the seizer is hurt or asleep) → `escaped`. Failure interrupts the target ("Kenji tries to seize you") |
| `release(person)` | instant | ends held |

## 4. Bodies, actions and physics

### 4.1 Speed caps (sim truth, `data/bodies.yaml`)
Ground speed = gait × age × load × health × energy × night ÷ route cost (biome × slope × road).

| Factor | Value |
|---|---|
| Gait (adult, flat, unloaded) | walk **1.40 m/s**, jog 2.5, run **3.5** (flee, `pace: "run"`), stalk 0.8 |
| Run endurance | ≤ 3 game minutes of run, then jog; running costs energy −1/min extra and doubles thirst |
| Age (02's table) | child 0.7, teen/young 1.0, middle 0.9, elder 0.6; children and elders never above jog |
| Load (carried kg / carry kg) | < 0.5 ×1.0; 0.5-0.9 ×0.85 (walk-carrying); ≥ 0.9 ×0.7 and no run |
| Health | ≤ 50 ×0.85; ≤ 20 ×0.5 (limp) |
| Energy | ≤ 10 ×0.5 (exists) |
| Night outside town, no fire within 12 m | ×0.8 |
| Slope (per route edge, grade g) | Tobler: cost × e^{3.5(\|g + 0.05\| − 0.05)}: flat 1.0, +10 % 1.42, +20 % 2.0 |
| Biome / ford / road / path / bridge | `TERRAIN_COST`; ford 1.5; road/path/bridge per §1.3 |

**Hard caps:** no citizen moves more than 210 m in a tick (run), 150 m (jog), 84 m (walk); no position change without
a route in the previous tick summary (spawn excepted). Hunt/attack stalks move along planned routes at 0.8 m/s
(no more 50 m straight jumps). Officers and thieves share run speed and endurance: a rested officer catches a tired
thief.

### 4.2 Drawing at ×1 / ×10 / ×60 / ×600 (supersedes the glide parts of D61, D72)
1. **Paths are streamed from where the motion starts.** A new walk, flee, follow or stalk sends its route at once
   (with its `action_started`), and every tick summary carries the path walked this tick plus the path ahead, with
   `speed_mps` and `gait`, for citizens, stalks and **animals**. (Audit F1: today the route is computed only after
   the tick's step, so 46 % of moves were never drawn as walking and ~500 teleports a day of 30-83 m happened.)
   The client walks each body from where it is drawn toward its goal at `speed_mps × rate` and stops at the goal.
2. **Time-lapse rule:** every clip and procedural motion (gaits, work, transitions, prop clips, smoke, doors, tree
   fall) plays at natural speed × `visualRate = min(rate, 10)`. Gait choice comes from game speed, never drawn
   speed, so ×1 and ×10 are stride-locked (no skating). Wind sway uses `min(rate, 3)`.
3. **Fast-forward mode** above ×10: gait clips capped at ×10, walkers trail their last 20 real seconds of drawn path
   (one merged `LineSegments`), the clock badge says "time-lapse ×60"; with nobody followed the camera eases to
   ≥ 150 m altitude.
4. Never teleport: snap threshold = `max(30, 2.5 × speed_mps × rate)` m; beyond it (tab hidden, reconnect) the body
   fades out/in over 0.3 s.
5. State changes that end a motion (death, pounce, arrive-and-sit) apply when the drawn body reaches the end of its
   path.

### 4.4 Ground contact (client, measured)
- Standing: feet on the ground under the origin. Seat: `Sit down` → `Sitting` → `Stand up`, hips on the slot's
  `height_m`, body faces the slot heading. Ground sit: `Sitting` + leg override (thighs −85°, hips 0.12 m).
- Lying (sleep, collapse, dead): pivot at the hips; pitch/roll from a plane fit to ground under head, hips and feet;
  lift = the rig's back depth (bind-pose bounds) + 0.02 m; frozen neutral pose + chest breathing (±1.5 % at 0.25 Hz),
  no playing Idle. Lie down / get up = 1.2 s roll. Dying = 0.8 s fall, knee buckle from `Hard landing`.
- Animals pitch to the slope (front and hind ground, ±25°); dead/sleeping pivot at the body centre.

- **Alert before flight:** a person within 2× flee distance → `alert` (head up, facing them). **Herd alarm:** when one
  member flees, every member of its group within 60 m flees on the same bearing ±15°. Stalking hunters aren't
  noticed at the flee distance.
- **Predator phases:** prowl → stalking (0.6 × walk until within pounce range: lion 25 m, wolf 30, bear 15, fox 10)
  → prey senses it (30 % per tick within 60 m, 100 % within 20 m) and flees → chasing. Solved inside the tick: caught
  iff gap ≤ (v_pred − v_prey) × min(burst_pred, 60 s); on a catch the predator pounces at the catch point and D68/D70's
  kill chance decides. Wolves catch over two ticks. A missed chase rests 60 min.
- **Kills leave remains:** a predator kill → `feeding` 60-120 min; then half the species' meat (rounded down) drops as

  threat to it: a pack of ≥ 2 holds, a lone wolf or fox leaves.
- **Waterholes:** herbivore groups drink 30 min at dawn and dusk at their hunting ground's waterhole: the ambush spot.
- **Dens:** predator homes pinned at the hotspot dens (cave mouth or outcrop + bone scatter).
- **Where animals live:** two thirds of herbivore groups are pinned to the hunting grounds of table 1.2 (≤ 700 m from a
  settlement), so people meet animals (audit F9: today some animal is within 50 m of a person in 1 % of ticks).

Procedural layers on baked Idle/Walk/Run: grazing (neck −55°, jaw 2 Hz), alert (head up, ears forward), stalking
(hips −20 %, head low), pouncing (0.5 s arc 0.5 m), bite (head lunge + jaw), rear (hips −50°), feeding, drinking,
sleeping (legs folded), dead (rolled 90°).

## 7. What citizens maximize (spectator scoreboard; never shown to minds as a score)
Derived per request by replay (D62) plus the daily `ledger`. Minds and brains only perceive the underlying facts in
words (purse, hunger, standing word); the numbers are for spectators and research.

| Axis | Fields | Definition |
|---|---|---|
| Wealth | `coins`, `wealth_mon` | coins + town value of inventory + equal share of the household chest |
| Flow | `income_24h`, `spent_24h` | coins in / out over the last game day |
| Health | `health`, `wellbeing_24h` | current health; % of last 24 h with hunger < 60, thirst < 60, energy > 20 |
| Duty | `tax_owed`, `tax_paid_7d` | household ledger |
| Status | `rank`, `standing` | rank (§3.2); standing in own group (§3.5) |
| Ambition | `ambition`, `progress` | the citizen's ambition and its world-checked progress (0-100 %) |
| Ties | `trade_partners_7d`, `people_known`, `bonds_top3` | distinct counterparties, known people |
| Skill | `skill_hours{}` | hours practised per skill (no effects in v1) |
| Society | `gini_wealth`, `money_supply_ok`, `deeds_7d`, `caught_7d` | per group; sanity flags |
